How to use your card

How do I use my digital card?

Open your personal card using the blue button. Tap the card to flip between the Pantry Packers logo and your contact details. Use Save Contact to add your information to another person's phone.

How do I share my card or QR code?

Copy your personal card link and paste it into an email, message or signature. For in-person meetings, show the QR code above or download the original image to print.

How can I update my contact information?

Contact the Pantry Packers card administrator to request changes. Your online card reads published information from the official system, so updates do not require sharing a new URL.

Who can help me with a special request?

Contact our card support contact using the Help button below and describe what you need.

Can another employee get a digital card?

Yes. Contact the card administrator with the employee's name, title and preferred business contact details so a new card can be prepared.

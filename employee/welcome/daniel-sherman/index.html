How do I use my digital business card?

Open your digital card using your personal link. Tap the card to flip it over and see your contact information.

  • Save Contact: Anyone who receives your card can save your contact details directly to their phone.
  • Quick contact options: The buttons beneath the digital card let people call, send an email or SMS, visit the Pantry Packers website, and open WhatsApp when available.
  • Sharing: Send your personal card link through WhatsApp, email, or text message.

Tip: Add your digital card link to your email signature so anyone you correspond with can save your details easily.

How can I share my card using a QR code?

Download your personal QR image using the button above and save it to your phone’s photo gallery.

  1. During an in-person meeting, open the QR image on your phone.
  2. Show it to the person you are meeting.
  3. Ask them to scan it using their phone’s camera.
  4. Your card will open on their device. They can then tap Save Contact to save your details.

This makes sharing all your contact information quick and easy, without typing anything manually.

Will I be able to edit my digital card?

A self-service system will be launched in the future so employees can manage and update their digital business cards independently. You’ll be able to update details such as your job title, phone numbers, email address, and profile photo.

Updates will appear on your existing digital card, so you won’t need to replace your link or share a new card.

Until the system launches, requests to update information will be handled manually.
